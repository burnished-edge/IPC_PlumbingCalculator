📌 Arizona Local Amendments & AHJ Code Footnotes (Click to Expand)

Statewide Code Structure: Arizona is a home-rule state with no statewide mandatory building code. Almost all Arizona municipalities adopt the International Building Code (IBC) and International Plumbing Code (IPC) (2018, 2021, or 2024 editions) for minimum fixture counts, with local administrative and technical amendments:

City of Phoenix (2024 IBC / IPC)

  • Group B Separate Facilities Threshold (IBC 2902.2 / IPC 403.2 Exception 3): Phoenix amends the single-user restroom exception for Business (Group B) occupancies to allow a single unisex restroom where total occupant load is 50 or fewer (raised from the base IBC/IPC limit of 25).
  • Drinking Fountains (IBC 2902.5 / IPC 410.2): Not required for occupancies with an occupant load of 30 or fewer, or in restaurants where water is served free of charge. Bottle-filling stations may substitute for up to 50% of required fountains.
  • Outdoor Patio Dining: Unenclosed outdoor dining areas generally must be included in plumbing fixture calculations unless exempted by specific local policy for small seasonal patios.

City of Scottsdale (2021 IBC / IPC)

  • Fixture Count Baseline: Uses unamended IBC Table 2902.1 / IPC Table 403.1 ratios.
  • Venting Restrictions (IPC Ch. 9 Amendment): Scottsdale strictly restricts Air Admittance Valves (AAVs / Studor vents); traditional atmospheric venting through the roof is required unless special approval is granted by the Building Official.
  • Water Efficiency: Strict desert conservation mandates apply to public lavatory faucets (0.5 gpm) and high-efficiency urinals/water closets.

City of Tucson & Pima County (2018 / 2024 IBC & IPC)

  • Fixture Count Baseline: Follows standard IBC Table 2902.1 / IPC Table 403.1 minimum ratios.
  • Commercial Rainwater & Graywater Conservation: Tucson enforces strict water harvesting and ultra-low-flow fixture mandates (1.28 gpf WCs, 0.125 gpf or waterless urinals in commercial applications).
  • Gender-Neutral / Single-Occupant Facilities: Permitted to count toward required Male or Female fixture totals in accordance with 2021/2024 IBC Section 2902.1.2.

Tempe, Mesa, Chandler & Gilbert (East Valley)

  • IBC 2902.2 Separate Facilities: Separate facilities are not required in structures or tenant spaces with a total occupant load (including employees and customers) of 15 or fewer, in Mercantile (Group M) up to 100 occupants, and Business (Group B) up to 25 occupants (or 50 if adopting 2024/Phoenix-aligned amendments).
  • Service Sinks (IBC 2902.3.3): A service sink is not required in Business (B) and Mercantile (M) tenant spaces with an occupant load of 15 or fewer (or where central building janitorial facilities are provided).
